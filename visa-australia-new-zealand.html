Proof that you can fund the trip and that you have reason to return home.

Depending on your situation

A few things that catch people out

  • Neither country puts a sticker in your passport. Your approval arrives by email and is linked electronically to the passport you applied with — so if you renew that passport before you fly, tell us immediately.
  • Australia may ask for a health examination or a police certificate depending on your history and how long you plan to stay.
  • New Zealand requires an NZeTA and an International Visitor Levy for eligible travellers, which is separate from the visitor visa. We'll tell you which route applies to you.

Photograph specification

  • 35 mm wide × 45 mm high, taken within the last 6 months.
  • Studio photograph on a plain light-grey or white background. No selfies, no scans of printed photos.
  • Full face, straight to camera, neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open and clearly visible.
  • Head and shoulders in frame, evenly lit, no shadows and no red-eye.
  • No headgear unless worn daily for religious reasons, and it must not obscure the face.
  • No sunglasses. Ordinary spectacles are best removed to avoid any glare.
  • For online submission, supply the digital file as well as prints — typically JPEG, under 10 MB.
If someone in Australia or New Zealand is inviting or funding your trip

Only needed when a relative or friend there is hosting you or covering part of your costs. Everything here comes from your sponsor.

  • Stating the purpose of your visit, their relationship to you, how long you'll stay, and which expenses they will cover.

  • Residence permit, permanent residency evidence, or citizenship certificate.

  • Birth certificate, marriage certificate, or family registration document.

  • Stating their position, salary and length of service. If self-employed, business registration plus proof of income instead.

  • Notice of Assessment in Australia, or the IRD income summary in New Zealand.

  • Rental agreement or property ownership papers. If they will not be hosting you, a hotel booking instead.
