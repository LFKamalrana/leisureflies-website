Money and tax

Proof that you can fund the trip yourself and that you have reason to return.

  • We recommend showing a closing balance of at least ₹4,00,000 per person. This is our guidance from experience, not a published Schengen minimum. Consulates look at the pattern as much as the number — a balance that has been there for months carries more weight than a recent lump sum.

  • D-Mat statements, LIC policies, mutual funds, fixed deposits, property papers.

Depending on your situation

Which country do you apply to?

Apply to the embassy of the country where you'll spend the most nights. If you're spending equal time in two or more, apply to the one you enter first. Getting this wrong is a common reason for rejection on a multi-country trip, and it's the first thing we check when we plan your itinerary.

Biometrics are taken in person and remain valid for 59 months, so if you've given them for a Schengen visa in the last five years, you may be able to skip that step.

Photograph specification

  • 35 mm wide × 45 mm high, no border, matt or semi-matt finish.
  • Taken within the last 6 months, in a studio. Selfies and scanned reprints are rejected.
  • Plain light-grey or off-white background, evenly lit, no shadow behind the head.
  • Full face, straight to camera, neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open and unobscured.
  • Face from chin to crown should fill roughly 70–80% of the frame height.
  • No headgear unless worn daily for religious reasons, and never covering the face.
  • Spectacles are best removed. If worn, frames must be thin, the eyes fully visible, and there must be no glare.
If someone in Europe is inviting or funding your trip

Only needed when a relative, friend or company in Europe is hosting you or paying part of your costs. Everything here comes from your sponsor.

  • Stating the purpose of your visit, their relationship to you, how long you'll stay, and which expenses they will cover. Some countries require this on an official form obtained from their local municipality — we'll tell you if yours does.

  • Residence permit, long-stay visa, or citizenship certificate.

  • Birth certificate, marriage certificate, or family registration document.

  • Stating their position, salary and length of service. If self-employed, business registration plus proof of income instead.

  • Rental agreement or property ownership papers. If they will not be hosting you, a hotel booking instead.

Planning more than one country?

Multi-country Europe trips are where most applications go wrong — wrong embassy, gaps in the hotel dates, insurance that doesn't cover the whole stay. Send us your plan and we'll build the file properly.
