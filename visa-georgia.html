What we need from you

All scans and digital files — nothing needs to be posted or handed over in person.

  • A clear, flat scan. Photographs of the passport taken on a phone are rejected.

  • Without spectacles — see the specification below. Studio photograph only.

  • The full number must be visible. A masked Aadhaar downloaded from the UIDAI site will not do.

  • Not mandatory, but applications where the contact details match the Aadhaar record are approved noticeably more often.

  • Any previous Schengen, UK, US, Canada, Australia or Japan visa. Strong travel history helps.

  • Read the rules below carefully — this is the item that decides most Georgia applications.

The bank statement rules, exactly

  • It must be a PDF downloaded directly from your bank's website or mobile app. A scan, a printout, or a print-to-PDF of a printed statement is not accepted.
  • Statements emailed to you by the bank are not accepted either. Log in and download it yourself.
  • It must cover 8 months. If your bank won't produce that range in one file, two consecutive statements together are fine.
  • The name on the statement must match the name in your passport exactly.
  • If the file is password-protected, that's fine — send us the password separately, alongside the encrypted file.
  • Minimum balance of ₹2,00,000 per person, excluding fixed deposits. If you're sponsoring your spouse or family, the balance must be ₹2,00,000 multiplied by the number of travellers.

Photograph specification

  • Digital colour photograph, taken within the last 6 months, studio only.
  • Plain white background, no border and no frame.
  • Full face, front view, eyes open, neutral expression.
  • The face should measure roughly 30–35 mm from chin to crown, with a 5–10 mm margin above the head.
  • No selfies and no scanned or photographed prints.
  • No head covering except where worn for religious reasons.
  • Spectacles off. The official guidance permits glasses without glare, but removing them avoids the most common cause of rejection entirely.

Your e-Visa can be refused purely on the photograph, with the fee lost. If in doubt, send us the file and we'll check it before submission.
