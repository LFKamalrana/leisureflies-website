How to make the best use of your cruise

This is where most first-time cruisers leave value on the table — we walk you through it before you book.

Choosing a Cabin

Match the room to how you'll actually use it.

  • Rarely in your room during the day? An interior or ocean-view cabin saves real money.
  • Traveling as a couple or for a honeymoon? A balcony suite is worth the upgrade.
  • Group or family trip? Adjoining cabins often work out cheaper than one large suite.
Choosing Activities

Book the popular ones early — don't wing it onboard.

  • Shows, speciality dining, and shore excursions fill up fast — we help you pre-book before sailing.
  • Traveling with kids? We flag the kids' club and family-friendly excursions upfront.
  • Want quieter days? We'll point you to the right sailing dates and itineraries.
