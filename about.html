Our Story

Built on trust, not templates.

LeisureFlies Travels began in 2010 in Chandigarh, when Kamal Rana — already known in the local travel circuit through the earlier Rana Travels — set out to build something more personal than a booking counter.

Fifteen years later, we're an IATA and TAAI accredited travel company that's helped over 10,000 travelers across 30+ countries plan flights, cruises, Eurail journeys, and full holiday packages — always with one philosophy: a real person handles your trip, start to finish.

We're not the biggest travel company. We're the one that still picks up the phone.

Our Journey

From Rana Travels to LeisureFlies.

A sequence, not a highlight reel — how the agency actually grew.

2010

LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd is founded

Kamal Rana launches LeisureFlies in Chandigarh, built around one idea — comprehensive travel programs planned around each client's actual needs, not off-the-shelf packages.

2013

IATA & TAAI accreditation earned

Formal industry accreditation cements LeisureFlies as a licensed, accountable travel operator — and unlocks access to discounted airfares and corporate-grade rates.

2016

Corporate & institutional travel begins

LeisureFlies starts partnering with corporates and schools — negotiated rates, credit facilities, and dedicated support for business and educational travel.

2021

We move to our own premises

LeisureFlies shifts to its own corporate office — SCO 70, Second Floor, Sector 38-C, Chandigarh — a milestone after over a decade of growth.

2023

Cruises, Eurail & luxury chauffeur services

Partnerships with major cruise lines, European rail passes, and a tie-up with Blacklane bring luxury chauffeur-driven cars in Dubai and Europe into what LeisureFlies can plan.

2026

A new chapter

A redesigned digital presence, built to match the standard of service clients already know us for.
