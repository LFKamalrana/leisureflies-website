Why Explore Bali

An ideal retreat for beaches, temples & rice terraces.

Adventurous, spiritual, or purely lazy-by-the-pool — Bali flexes to whatever holiday you're after. It's home to the coral reefs of Tulamben, the mountain peaks of Kintamani, the buzz of Seminyak and Kuta, and the quieter, traditional village life of Ubud.

Between the sunrises over Mount Batur and the sunsets at Uluwatu, it's a destination that's hard to leave once you've landed.

Must-Do Experiences

Things to do in Bali.

The tours our travelers ask for most, built into every LeisureFlies Bali package.

Nusa Penida Island Tour

One of the most beautiful islands in the world, reached by boat — home to the iconic Broken Beach.

Bedugul & Tanah Lot Tour

Visit the exotic Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, set on a high tableland surrounded by ponds.

Denpasar City & Bali Bounty Cruise

Explore Bali's architecture, then take to the ocean for snorkeling, banana boat rides, and a 44-metre water slide.

Kintamani Volcano & Ubud Village

View Mount Batur and the Ubud Tegalalang rice terraces, then visit Celuk's gold & silver craft village and the Holy Spring Temple.

Watersports & Uluwatu Temple

Parasailing, banana boat and jet ski rides, the roaring Water Blow at Nusa Dua, and Uluwatu's cliffside temple with Kecak fire dance.

Ubud Art Market & Villages

Shop the Ubud Art Market and visit Celuk & Mas villages, known for centuries-old craft traditions.

Indonesia Visa

Getting a visa for Indonesia is refreshingly simple — Indian travelers don't need to apply in advance. A Visa on Arrival is purchased directly at the immigration hall on landing in Bali (or anywhere in Indonesia), and it's valid for 30 days.

Best time to visit?

Between April and October.

Temple etiquette

Don't enter temples with your shoes on, and avoid using your left hand when giving or receiving gifts.

Bali in Numbers

Kopi Luwak

The world's most expensive coffee comes from Bali — it's not your typical coffee bean.

3,000+ fish species

A local tradition

Balinese babies aren't allowed to touch the ground for their first few months — they're considered little angels.
