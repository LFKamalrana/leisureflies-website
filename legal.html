Privacy Policy

LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through leisureflies.com and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit through enquiry forms — name, phone number, email address.
  • Trip details you share with us — destination, travel dates, number of travelers, budget preferences.
  • Documents you send us for bookings — passport copies, PAN card, photographs — only when required for visa or ticketing purposes.
  • Basic website usage data (pages visited, general location) via standard analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare travel quotations.
  • To process bookings with airlines, hotels, cruise lines, and other travel suppliers.
  • To send you booking confirmations, itinerary updates, and relevant offers (you can opt out anytime).
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Sharing

We share necessary booking details (name, passport info, etc.) only with the specific airlines, hotels, cruise lines, visa processors, or other suppliers required to fulfil your booking. We use WhatsApp Business for communication convenience — messages sent there are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet. Please avoid sending sensitive documents over unsecured channels where possible.

Terms of Service

By using this website or engaging LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd for travel services, you agree to the following terms.

Website Content

Package prices, itineraries, and availability shown on this website are indicative and subject to change without notice. Final pricing and inclusions will always be confirmed in writing before you make a payment.

Bookings

A booking is confirmed only once we receive your written confirmation and the applicable booking amount. LeisureFlies acts as a booking agent for third-party suppliers (airlines, hotels, cruise lines, rail operators) — the respective supplier's own terms and conditions also apply to your booking.

Liability

LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd is not liable for delays, cancellations, or changes caused by airlines, hotels, weather, government regulations, visa authorities, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellation charges vary by supplier (airline, hotel, cruise line, tour operator) and will be communicated at the time of booking confirmation. As a general guide:

  • Cancellations made well in advance of travel typically incur lower charges than last-minute cancellations.
  • Some bookings (promotional airfares, certain hotel rates) may be fully non-refundable — this will always be disclosed before you pay.
  • Refunds, where applicable, are processed after the respective supplier releases the refunded amount to us, which can take several weeks depending on the supplier.
