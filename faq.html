Questions we get asked most

Accreditation, visas, pricing, cancellations and how we actually work. If your question is not here, message us and a specialist will answer it directly.

LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd is an IATA and TAAI accredited travel agency based at SCO 70, Sector 38-C, Chandigarh, operating since 2010. Below are straight answers to what people ask before booking.

Is LeisureFlies Travels a licensed travel agency?

Yes. LeisureFlies Travels Pvt Ltd has operated from Chandigarh since 2010 and has been IATA and TAAI accredited since 2013. IATA accreditation is what gives us direct access to airline inventory and negotiated fares rather than reselling through another agent.

Where is your office and when are you open?

SCO 70, 2nd Floor, Sector 38-C, Chandigarh 160014. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and closed on Sundays. Clients who are already travelling with us can reach us on WhatsApp outside those hours.

Which destinations do you cover?

We have detailed packages for fourteen destinations: Bali, Thailand, Dubai, Singapore, Vietnam, the Maldives, Mauritius, Sri Lanka and Saudi Arabia internationally, and Goa, Kerala, Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh and the Andaman Islands domestically. We also arrange Europe by rail, cruises worldwide, and one-off trips to destinations not listed here.

How much does a holiday package cost?

It depends on your travel dates, hotel category, number of travellers and how much sightseeing is built in, so we quote each trip individually rather than publishing one fixed price. As a rough guide, Dubai and Thailand start around ₹42,000–₹50,000 per person and Bali around ₹58,000, on twin sharing. The written quote you receive is the amount you pay — we do not add convenience fees at the end.

Do you offer a price match?

Yes. If you find the same product cheaper elsewhere, send us a screenshot within five minutes of receiving our quote. It has to show a date and time stamp, and be for the identical product — same dates, same hotel category, same inclusions — from a publicly bookable source. It does not apply once a booking is confirmed and paid.

Do you handle visas?

Visa documentation is part of the booking service. Requirements vary: Thailand, Mauritius and the Maldives are visa-on-arrival for Indian passport holders, Bali is visa-on-arrival valid 30 days, Dubai and Vietnam are online e-visas, Sri Lanka needs an ETA applied in advance, and Singapore requires a pre-applied visa. We tell you exactly what to send and handle the application.

Can you book only flights, without a package?

Yes. We book domestic and international flights on both full-service and low-cost carriers at pre-negotiated fares. Plenty of our clients book only tickets.

What cruises can you book?

Cordelia Cruises for domestic sailings out of Mumbai, Goa, Chennai and Kochi, and all the major international lines including Royal Caribbean, MSC, Costa, Norwegian, Disney, Celebrity, Silversea, Holland America, Princess and Viking. Pricing matches booking direct, and often beats it.

Is a Eurail Pass cheaper than buying individual train tickets?

Not always, and we will tell you when it is not. Two or three short hops between neighbouring cities usually work out cheaper as point-to-point tickets. Five or more travel days across several countries is where a pass starts to pay for itself. Send us your route and we price both options before you commit.

Do you arrange vegetarian, Jain or Indian food abroad?

Yes, and we confirm it in writing before booking. Cordelia Cruises runs pure-vegetarian counters and dedicated Jain meals. Most Maldives resorts now offer vegetarian, Indian and Jain options. On group tours we arrange Indian food at each destination.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellation charges are set by the supplier — the airline, hotel, cruise line or operator — and are disclosed at the time of booking. Cancelling well ahead of travel costs less than cancelling late. Some promotional airfares and hotel rates are entirely non-refundable, and we always tell you before you pay. Refunds are processed once the supplier releases the money to us, which can take several weeks.

How do I pay, and when?

A booking is confirmed once we have your written confirmation and the booking amount. Payment terms depend on the supplier and how far ahead you are travelling. The team sets this out in writing before you transfer anything.

Do you handle corporate and school travel?

Yes. Corporate accounts get negotiated rates, credit facilities and a dedicated contact. School picnics and educational tours are planned around safety and logistics so teachers can focus on the students rather than the arrangements.

Can you arrange airport transfers and chauffeur-driven cars?

Yes. Through our partnership with Blacklane we arrange chauffeur-driven vehicles in Dubai and across Europe — Mercedes-Benz S, E and C Class, BMW 7, 5 and 3 Series, Audi A8 and A6, Bentley, Cadillac Escalade and Toyota Land Cruiser. Self-driven sports cars are available in Dubai and Monaco.

What support do I get once the trip has started?

You keep the same specialist throughout, reachable on WhatsApp for the duration of the trip. Flight delays, hotel problems, visa questions at immigration — that is what the number is for.

How quickly will you reply to an enquiry?

Within a few hours during office hours, Monday to Saturday. If you would rather not wait, WhatsApp us on +91 98149 10087 and someone will pick it up directly.
